> **About CustomBuffers**  
> Custom Buffers is a mod made by [chromehaert](https://mcpedl.com/user/chromehaert/) that enhances RenderDragon shader capabilities by allowing them to get more information from the game.  
> This lets shaders to implement features like dynamic shadows, true water reflection and much more. Currently, it's only available on Android.
> 
> CustomBuffers is built into MB Loader since v2.12, make sure to enable it from settings!
>
> Please keep in mind that it's upto the shader developer to utilize the mod. The mod on it's own can't do anything.
